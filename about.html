Our pub

Old school, warm, well-run.

The Alma is a Challenge 25 venue with a clear zero-tolerance approach to abuse, drugs and behaviour that spoils the pub for other guests. That clarity is part of what keeps the atmosphere comfortable and trusted.

Weekly entertainment includes Friday DJs, Saturday singers, Sunday singers, fortnightly Thursday karaoke and fortnightly Monday quiz nights.

House notes

  • Dartboard available, booking recommended
  • Hot desk enquiries available
