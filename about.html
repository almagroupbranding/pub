About The Alma

A proper local with standards.

The Alma is an old-school Sidcup pub with soul, garden, entertainment, fresh food, community support and clear house standards.

Character

Warm welcome, clear rules, better trust.

The Alma is proud to be an LGBTQ+ friendly venue and aims to create a safe, friendly environment for everyone who respects the pub, the staff and other guests.

Some house rules exist because the venue is licensed and owner-led. They should be presented clearly and calmly so good customers feel reassured rather than put off.

  • No children under 14, including babies, in any areas of the pub or garden.
  • No dogs/pets in the pub or garden.
  • Challenge 25 applies.
  • Zero tolerance for abuse, phobia or drug use.
